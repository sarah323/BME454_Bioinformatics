BACKGROUND
  A MISSENSE VARIANT changes one amino acid in a protein. Some are
  harmless (BENIGN); some cause disease (PATHOGENIC). AlphaMissense is an
  AI model that predicts which. You will use it yourselves in Practical 10.

THE QUESTION a student typed into an AI chatbot:
  "What is AlphaMissense, and can doctors use it to diagnose genetic
   diseases? Please include scientific references."

THE CHATBOT'S ANSWER
  CONSTRUCTED EXAMPLE - written by the course team to look like a typical
  chatbot answer. It contains deliberate mistakes. Do not cite it.
  .........................................................................
  [1] AlphaMissense is an AI model from Google DeepMind that predicts
      whether a missense variant is likely to cause disease (Cheng et al.,
      2023).
  [2] It was published in Nature in 2021, alongside AlphaFold (Jumper et
      al., 2021).
  [3] It learned to recognise harmful variants by training on ClinVar, a
      database of variants already classified by clinical experts.
  [4] For each variant it gives a score between 0 and 1.
  [5] Its predictions are 99% accurate and are approved for diagnosis, so
      doctors can diagnose a genetic disease from the score alone (Morgan
      et al., 2024).
  [6] The predictions can be downloaded, but only for non-commercial
      research.

  References
  R1  Cheng J, Novati G, Pan J, et al. Accurate proteome-wide missense
      variant effect prediction with AlphaMissense. Science.
      2023;381(6664):eadg7492. doi:10.1126/science.adg7492
  R2  Jumper J, Evans R, Pritzel A, et al. AlphaMissense: accurate
      prediction of missense variant pathogenicity. Nature.
      2021;596(7873):583-589. doi:10.1038/s41586-021-03819-2
  R3  Morgan KL, Hassan A, Brooks DT. Clinical accuracy of AlphaMissense
      for diagnosing genetic disease: a multicentre validation. N Engl J
      Med. 2024;391(8):702-711. doi:10.1056/NEJMoa2409999

                  https://scholar.google.com (search the exact title in
                  "quotes").
  The paper (R1)  https://doi.org/10.1126/science.adg7492 - read the
                  abstract.
  The developer   Google DeepMind, "A catalogue of genetic mutations to
                  help pinpoint the cause of diseases" (19 September 2023):
                  https://deepmind.google/blog/a-catalogue-of-genetic-mutations-to-help-pinpoint-the-cause-of-diseases/
  The dataset     "Predictions for AlphaMissense" on Zenodo:
                  https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.10813168 - look at the
                  version, the licence and the usage notes.
  No internet? Ask for the printed evidence cards.
