# How to Use This Repository

The practical materials will be organized and uploaded **weekly** throughout the course.

## For Each Practical

### 1. Start with the Weekly Worksheet

The worksheet contains the instructions, activities, questions, and resources needed for that week's practical.

### 2. Follow the Activities in the Order Provided

Some activities may involve external bioinformatics tools, databases, datasets, or other online resources.

### 3. Document Your Work When Required

Keep track of the tools, resources, datasets, and important steps used during your work.

### 4. Complete the Provenance Log When Required

The **Provenance Log** is used to document the origin and use of relevant data, resources, tools, and outputs.

You do **not** need to complete the Provenance Log for every activity. Complete it only when instructed to do so in the relevant worksheet.

### 5. Keep Your Work Organized

Follow the file-naming, folder, and submission instructions provided in each worksheet.

### 6. Review Your Results Critically

Obtaining a result is not the end of the activity. Consider:

- Is the result biologically meaningful?
- Was the method appropriate for the question?
- What are the limitations of the approach?
- Are there potential sources of error or uncertainty?
- Can the result be reproduced or independently verified?

## Weekly Workflow

For each practical, follow this general workflow:

**Worksheet → Activities → Documentation → Provenance Log (when required) → Review & Interpretation → Submission**

> **Important:** Instructions may vary between practicals. Always follow the specific requirements provided in the weekly worksheet.
