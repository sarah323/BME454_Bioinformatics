# Biomedical Engineering 454 — Bioinformatics

**Faculty of Medicine, Alexandria University**

This repository contains the practical materials, activities, and resources for **Biomedical Engineering 454: Bioinformatics**.

The course introduces Biomedical Engineering students to the principles and applications of **bioinformatics, computational biology, genomics, and biological data analysis**. It combines practical exploration of bioinformatics tools with critical thinking and scientific interpretation.

## Course Focus

The practical sessions cover topics including:

- Bioinformatics and computational biology
- Genomics and biological data analysis
- Sequence analysis and interpretation
- Bioinformatics tools and online resources
- Analysis and interpretation of biological datasets
- Artificial intelligence in bioinformatics and biomedical research
- Reproducible and transparent research practices
- Data organization, metadata, and file management
- Critical appraisal of computational biology research
- Applications of bioinformatics in biomedical and clinical research

## Learning Approach

The course emphasizes **understanding and critical thinking**, rather than programming alone. Students will work with real or representative biological data and computational tools to explore how bioinformatics is used in modern biomedical research.

Students will also learn to:

- Select and use appropriate computational resources
- Interpret bioinformatics results in a biological context
- Evaluate the strengths and limitations of computational approaches
- Assess the reproducibility and transparency of published analyses
- Use AI tools responsibly and critically
- Document computational workflows and research decisions

## Repository Contents

This repository may include:

- Practical exercises
- Step-by-step guides
- Example datasets
- Supporting resources
- Activity instructions
- Templates and documentation
- Links to external bioinformatics tools and databases

## Intended Audience

This repository is designed for students enrolled in **Biomedical Engineering 454 — Bioinformatics** at the Faculty of Medicine, Alexandria University.

## Responsible Use

Students are expected to use computational and AI-based tools as **learning and research aids**, while maintaining scientific integrity. AI-generated content and computational results should be critically evaluated, verified, and appropriately documented.
